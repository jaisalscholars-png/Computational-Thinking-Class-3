📘 CBSE Computational Thinking Guide

Computational Thinking (CT) is NOT about staring at a computer screen all day. It is a way of thinking to solve problems in real life!

1. Decomposition: Breaking down big chores (like cleaning your entire bedroom) into small steps (pick up books, fold blankets, sweep floor).
2. Pattern Recognition: Spotting repeats and similarities to solve puzzles faster (like counting by 2s or traffic lights cycles).
3. Algorithms: Creating an ordered set of instructions so a friend or machine can replicate your result (like a cake recipe).
4. Debugging: Finding mistakes when things don't go as planned and happily fixing them!
